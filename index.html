Directions For Use

  1. Wet hair thoroughly with lukewarm water.
  2. Pump a generous amount of Pure11 Shampoo into your palms.
  3. Massage gently into wet hair and scalp until a rich lather forms.
  4. Rinse thoroughly. Repeat if desired for extra deep cleansing.

Cautions & Storage

  • For external use only.
  • Avoid direct contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse immediately with clean water.
  • Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
